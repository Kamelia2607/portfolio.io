About Me

Hello, my name is Kamelia, I'm a Data Science graduate student at Old Dominion University, concentrating in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence. I earned my B.S. in Data Science in May 2026 and am pursuing my Masters, expected in May 2027.

My recent work sees me building technical pipelines that convert complex data into actionable insight. This includes developing clustering and visualization workflows for agent-based models, creating LLM-driven storytelling systems for simulation outputs, and building retrieval-augmented logbook analysis tools using semantic search and AI-assisted reporting techniques. I've also worked on hybrid optimization for particle accelerators used in experimental nuclear physics, exploring how Bayesian optimization and reinforcement learning can complement one another to improve the control of complex scientific processes. I have experience working across the full analytical pipeline, including data cleaning, normalization, feature engineering, clustering, visualization, Bayesian optimization, model evaluation, and technical implementation using Python and modern machine learning tooling. I particularly enjoy projects that combine machine learning with practical software systems and human-centered interpretation. In 2025, I was part of the winning team at the Hampton Roads Datathon, where we developed a deep learning pipeline leveraging probabilistic data structures and spatial signals to predict mosquito species presence and communicate the results to a non-technical public health panel. Technical interests include machine learning, AI systems, Bayesian optimization, reinforcement learning, semantic retrieval, simulation analysis, clustering workflows, and AI-assisted analytical tooling.
